\begin{abstract}
Phishing attacks have rapidly evolved beyond simple textual lures, spanning multimodal optical vectors (Quishing / QR phishing), weaponized web uniform resource identifiers (URLs), and semi-structured communications (emails) that evade perimeter filters, Secure Email Gateways (SEGs), and textual NLP parsers. In this paper, we conduct a systematic empirical and theoretical study of multimodal neural architectures and evidential uncertainty quantification across all three primary attack channels, presenting \textbf{PhishShield-EDL}. We formulate modular visual-lexical neural architectures for optical 2D barcodes (\textbf{Quish-EDL}), dual lexical-tabular neural architectures for standalone URL inspection (\textbf{URL-EDL}), and header-content neural architectures for full email triage (\textbf{Email-EDL}) under strict encoder parity and identical training budgets.

Across 5-fold cross-validation on rigorous multi-source benchmarks (3,000 paired QR codes, 6,000 balanced URLs, and 10,000 balanced Enron + Nazario emails), our proposed early fusion architectures achieve state-of-the-art detection performance: $94.41\% \pm 0.44\%$ on QR barcodes ($0.9885$ ROC-AUC, $+7.52$ percentage point ROC-AUC gain over published structural QR baselines), $90.67\% \pm 0.58\%$ on URLs (statistically equivalent to a 34-feature Random Forest baseline, $p = 0.0728$), and $98.19\% \pm 0.19\%$ on emails (statistically equivalent between evidential and cosine models, $p = 0.1388$). This establishes zero accuracy penalty for equipping tri-modal phishing classification with evidential uncertainty quantification.

Beyond classification accuracy, we investigate the calibration and out-of-distribution (OOD) uncertainty properties of Subjective Logic Evidential Deep Learning (EDL), demonstrating three key empirical and theoretical findings: (1)~\textbf{In-Distribution Underconfidence}: while deterministic early fusion with softmax achieves near-optimal in-distribution calibration (ECE $= 0.0236$ for QR, $0.0301$ for URL, $0.0063$ for Email), Dirichlet evidential parameterization suffers from systematic underconfidence (ECE $= 0.0871$ for QR, $0.0979$ for URL, $0.0640$ for Email) driven by Dirichlet KL evidence suppression; (2)~\textbf{First Cross-Modal Parity Proof of Spurious Evidence Leakage}: while out-of-distribution overconfidence in evidential models has been theoretically critiqued (Bengs et al., 2022; Ulmer et al., 2023), we provide the first cross-modal empirical proof that canonical binary Dirichlet EDL ($e = \text{ReLU}(z)$) replicates spurious evidence leakage on zero-day attacks across 2D optical QR matrices (inverted ratio $\mathbf{0.82\times \pm 0.04\times}$), 1D lexical-tabular URLs (inverted ratio $\mathbf{0.73\times \pm 0.08\times}$), and natural email text (spurious Dirichlet strength $S_{\text{OOD}} = 8.61 \gg 2.0$, detecting only $11.88\%$ of zero-day threats at $p_{95}$); and (3)~\textbf{Tri-Modal Restoration via Outlier Exposure and Cosine Heads}: we demonstrate that bounding latent feature norms via Orthogonal Cosine Normalization and regularizing uninformative Dirichlet boundaries via Outlier Exposure (OE) universally restores zero-day epistemic uncertainty elevation ($3.80\times$ on QR, $2.81\times$ on URLs, and $3.50\times$ on Emails with $95.28\%$ zero-day detection), while achieving superior Brier score calibration ($0.0690$ on URLs, beating Softmax $0.0766$).

\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Traditional enterprise email security relies on Static Heuristic Analysis, Domain Name System Blacklists (DNSBL), and Natural Language Processing (NLP) parsers to inspect email headers, text bodies, and embedded hyperlinks. However, cyber adversaries have increasingly pivoted toward evasion vectors that subvert standard inspection pipelines:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Quishing (QR Phishing)}: Adversaries render weaponized URLs as 2D optical barcodes within PDF attachments or image bodies, bypassing perimeter text inspection and shifting the scan to unmanaged mobile devices~\cite{fbi2024}.
    \item \textbf{Obfuscated and Zero-Day URLs}: Adversaries deploy algorithmic domain generation, multi-hop open redirects, brand typosquatting, and fast-flux cloud hosting (PaaS) to evade static blacklists and signature rules.
\end{enumerate}

Because target URIs inside QR codes are rendered as optical module matrices, text-based parsers perceive the email as benign text. Similarly, novel URL transport evasion protocols (such as IPFS gateways or PaaS tunneling) generate zero-day distribution shifts that trigger high-confidence misclassifications in standard deep networks.

This paper addresses the fundamental architectural, generalization, and calibration questions in multimodal and sequence-based phishing defense:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Multimodal Visual-Lexical Superiority in Quishing}: We systematically evaluate a 6-rung ablation ladder (M1: Vision-Only, M2: Lexical-Only, M3: Early Fusion Softmax, M4: Late Fusion, M5: Cross-Attention, M6: Evidential Early Fusion) under strict encoder parity, demonstrating that early multimodal concatenation ($94.41\% \pm 0.44\%$, $0.9885$ ROC-AUC) statistically outperforms unimodal and complex co-attention baselines.
    \item \textbf{Dual-Stream URL Architecture \& Zero-Cost Epistemic Parity}: We formulate URL-EDL, combining 1D character convolutions with a 34-feature scaled tabular projection. On a 5-fold ablation ladder (U1--U6), URL-EDL achieves $90.67\% \pm 0.58\%$ (and $90.27\%$ with Cosine-OE), statistically equivalent to the 34-feature Random Forest baseline ($91.50\% \pm 0.38\%$, McNemar $p = 0.0728$), demonstrating zero accuracy penalty for equipping URL classification with epistemic uncertainty.
    \item \textbf{Cross-Modal Empirical Proof of Spurious Evidence Leakage}: Moving beyond single-modality observations, we demonstrate that canonical binary Dirichlet EDL ($e = \text{ReLU}(z)$) replicates spurious evidence leakage on unseen zero-day attacks across both discrete 2D optical QR matrices ($0.82\times \pm 0.04\times$) and 1D lexical-tabular URL sequences ($0.73\times \pm 0.08\times$), formally explaining why standard evidential loss produces lower uncertainty on OOD attacks than on in-distribution samples ($\bar{u}_{\text{OOD}} < \bar{u}_{\text{ID}}$).
    \item \textbf{Architectural Resolution via Cosine Heads and Outlier Exposure}: We demonstrate that Outlier Exposure and Annealed Cosine Normalization reliably eliminate spurious evidence leakage across both modalities, elevating zero-day uncertainty to $>2.81\times\text{--}3.80\times$ (with OOD median uncertainty $= 1.0$) while achieving the best Brier score loss ($0.0690$ on URLs, outperforming Softmax $0.0766$).
    \item \textbf{Cross-Dataset Generalization \& Operational Serving Framework}: We validate cross-dataset generalization across three independent benchmarks (Trad et al., CIC-Trap4Phish 2025, Galadima 2025) and establish an operational architecture that deploys calibrated deterministic models for live inference while maintaining evidential models in shadow mode for triage and poison-resilient active learning.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Related Work}
\subsection{QR Code Security \& Quishing Analysis}
The security implications of 2D optical barcodes have been studied across physical and digital attack surfaces. Early work by Krombholz et al.~\cite{krombholz2014} provided the first survey of QR code vulnerabilities and usable security. Vidas et al.~\cite{vidas2013} conducted seminal empirical studies under the term ``QRishing,'' demonstrating user susceptibility to unscanned QR lures. Amoah \& Hayfron-Acquah~\cite{amoah2022} analyzed mobile quishing attack vectors and mitigation strategies.

Recent detection efforts have focused on isolated pipeline stages:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Optical Barcode Structural Analysis}: Trad \& Chehab~\cite{trad2025} directly classified optical QR code structures and geometric module matrices using machine learning classifiers (achieving ROC-AUC of $0.9106\text{--}0.9133$ with XGBoost) without extracting or parsing the underlying payload URLs. While this avoids decoding dependencies, it remains blind to rich lexical indicators within the destination URI.
    \item \textbf{Visual Structural \& Geometry Analysis}: Galadima~\cite{galadima2025} published the Mendeley benchmark dataset of 1,000 verified benign and malicious QR codes (DOI: 10.17632/cmhh7744sp.1), evaluating visual encoding density and error correction variations. Agrawal et al.~\cite{agrawal2021} investigated visual watermarking in QR matrices.
    \item \textbf{Large-Scale Multi-Format Datasets}: The Canadian Institute for Cybersecurity released CIC-Trap4Phish~\cite{cic2025}, aggregating multi-format phishing lures across emails, QR attachments, and documents.
\end{itemize}
Our framework bridges this division by evaluating visual barcode structures, decoded payload sequences, and lexical-tabular representations under unified multimodal fusion.

\subsection{Multimodal Neural Architectures in Phishing}
Multimodal architectures have gained traction in webpage phishing detection (e.g., Phishpedia~\cite{lin2021}, VisualPhishNet~\cite{abdelnabi2020}, PhishIntention~\cite{liu2024}, Sahoo et al.~\cite{sahoo2017}). In quishing, visual inputs consist of discrete 2D frequency patterns rather than natural webpage screenshots. We systematically evaluate whether bilinear cross-attention or early fusion concatenation provides superior inductive bias for optical barcode representations.

\subsection{Evidential Deep Learning \& Uncertainty Quantification}
Standard deep classifiers output uncalibrated Softmax distributions that frequently exhibit overconfidence on out-of-distribution (OOD) inputs~\cite{guo2017}. To quantify epistemic uncertainty in safety-critical domains without Bayesian sampling overhead, Sensoy et al.~\cite{sensoy2018} proposed Evidential Deep Learning (EDL) using Subjective Logic Dirichlet priors. 

While extensions such as Prior Networks~\cite{malinin2018}, Posterior Networks~\cite{charpentier2020}, and open-set evidential recognition~\cite{bao2021} have been explored, critical theoretical vulnerabilities in evidential uncertainty have been identified: Bengs et al.~\cite{bengs2022} revealed fundamental pitfalls in Dirichlet second-order uncertainty quantification under epistemic distribution shifts, and Ulmer et al.~\cite{ulmer2023} highlighted that evidential models can exhibit uncalibrated overconfidence on out-of-distribution tabular and structured data. Hendrycks et al.~\cite{hendrycks2019} established that Outlier Exposure (OE) with auxiliary non-target data provides a principled mechanism for regularizing anomaly detection models. While OOD overconfidence in evidential models has been observed theoretically, our work provides the first rigorous cross-modal empirical proof that canonical binary Dirichlet EDL replicates inverted epistemic uncertainty ($u_{\text{OOD}} < u_{\text{ID}}$ at $0.73\times\text{--}0.82\times$) across discrete 2D optical matrices and continuous 1D lexical-tabular sequences under strict experimental parity, and demonstrates its durable architectural resolution via Outlier Exposure and Cosine-Normalized evidential heads.

\subsection{Adversarial Dataset Poisoning \& Multi-Source Consensus}
Adaptive security models that continually fine-tune on live traffic are vulnerable to adversarial poisoning attacks~\cite{biggio2012, carlini2024}. Semi-supervised label bootstrapping frameworks such as Snorkel~\cite{ratner2017} address this by fusing multiple weak supervision sources. We incorporate these principles to design an active learning triage protocol with strict poison-prevention firewalls.

\section{Theoretical Formulation \& Architecture}

\subsection{Visual, Lexical, and Tabular Feature Extractors}
To guarantee rigorous comparative validity across all ablation rungs, modular feature encoders are utilized:

\subsubsection{Spatial QR Visual Stream ($\mathbf{f}_{\text{vis}}$)}
Given a normalized 2D grayscale QR matrix $\mathbf{X}_{\text{vis}} \in \mathbb{R}^{64 \times 64 \times 1}$, spatial features are extracted:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{H} = \text{MaxPool}_{4\times 4}(\text{ReLU}(\text{Conv2D}_{16}(\mathbf{X}_{\text{vis}})))
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{f}_{\text{vis}} = \text{Dropout}_{0.2}(\text{ReLU}(\text{Dense}_{32}(\text{Flatten}(\mathbf{H})))) \in \mathbb{R}^{32}
\end{equation}

\subsubsection{Lexical URL Sequence Stream ($\mathbf{f}_{\text{lex}}$)}
Given a character sequence $\mathbf{X}_{\text{lex}} \in \mathbb{Z}^{180}$, embedding vectors $\mathbf{E} \in \mathbb{R}^{180 \times 32}$ are processed through a 1D convolutional feature extractor:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{L} = \text{MaxPool}_6(\text{ReLU}(\text{Conv1D}_{32, k=3}(\mathbf{E})))
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{f}_{\text{lex}} = \text{Dropout}_{0.2}(\text{ReLU}(\text{Dense}_{32}(\text{Flatten}(\mathbf{L})))) \in \mathbb{R}^{32}
\end{equation}

\subsubsection{Scaled Tabular Feature Stream ($\mathbf{f}_{\text{tab}}$)}
In standalone URL analysis, in addition to the 180-token character lexical sequence $\mathbf{X}_{\text{lex}}$, we extract $D=34$ statistical, structural, and lexical features $\mathbf{X}_{\text{tab}} \in \mathbb{R}^{34}$ (including domain entropy, path length, token distributions, sub-domain depth, and suspicious keyword flags). To prevent numerical scale divergence and feature dominance during fusion, tabular features are strictly normalized using a fold-isolated \texttt{StandardScaler} ($\boldsymbol{\mu}_{\text{train}}, \boldsymbol{\sigma}_{\text{train}}$) fit exclusively on the training fold:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{f}_{\text{tab}} = \text{Dropout}_{0.2}(\text{ReLU}(\text{Dense}_{32}(\text{StandardScaler}(\mathbf{X}_{\text{tab}})))) \in \mathbb{R}^{32}
\end{equation}

\subsubsection{Early Fusion Representations}
For Quish-EDL, visual and lexical features are fused via concatenation:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{h}_{\text{fused}} = [\mathbf{f}_{\text{vis}} \,\|\, \mathbf{f}_{\text{lex}}] \in \mathbb{R}^{64}
\end{equation}
For URL-EDL, sequence and tabular embeddings are concatenated under identical downstream dimensionality:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{h}_{\text{url}} = [\mathbf{f}_{\text{lex}} \,\|\, \mathbf{f}_{\text{tab}}] \in \mathbb{R}^{64}
\end{equation}
Both $\mathbf{h}_{\text{fused}}$ and $\mathbf{h}_{\text{url}}$ feed into identical classification necks ($\text{Dense}_{48} \to \text{Dropout}_{0.2} \to \text{Dense}_{32}$) followed by either deterministic Softmax or evidential Dirichlet heads.

\subsection{Subjective Logic Evidential Head}
The network maps the joint representation to non-negative evidence $\mathbf{e} = \text{ReLU}(\mathbf{z}) \ge 0$, inducing a Dirichlet distribution parameterized by concentration parameters:
\begin{equation}
\alpha_k = e_k + 1, \quad k \in \{1, 2\}
\end{equation}
Total Dirichlet strength $S = \sum_{k=1}^K \alpha_k = e_1 + e_2 + K$. The belief mass $b_k$ and epistemic uncertainty mass $u$ satisfy the Subjective Logic axiom:
\begin{equation}
u + \sum_{k=1}^K b_k = 1, \quad \text{where } b_k = \frac{\alpha_k - 1}{S} = \frac{e_k}{S}, \quad u = \frac{K}{S} \in (0, 1]
\end{equation}
The loss function incorporates an epoch-annealed KL divergence penalty on misleading evidence against a flat uniform Dirichlet prior:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}(\boldsymbol{\alpha}, \mathbf{y}) = \sum_{k=1}^K \left(y_k - \frac{\alpha_k}{S}\right)^2 + \frac{\alpha_k (S - \alpha_k)}{S^2 (S + 1)} + \lambda_t \text{KL}\left[\text{Dir}(\tilde{\boldsymbol{\alpha}}) \,\|\, \text{Dir}(\mathbf{1})\right]
\end{equation}
where $\tilde{\boldsymbol{\alpha}} = \mathbf{y} + (1 - \mathbf{y}) \odot \boldsymbol{\alpha}$ removes misleading evidence on the non-ground-truth class, with half-budget linear warmup schedule $\lambda_t = \min(1.0, t / 4)$ over $T=8$ training epochs.

\subsection{Mitigating Spurious Evidence Leakage via Outlier Exposure and Angular Margins}
To prevent unconstrained feature activations from emitting spurious positive evidence ($\mathbf{e} > 0$) on novel out-of-distribution domains, we formulate two architectural remedies:

\subsubsection{Dirichlet Prior Regularization with Outlier Exposure (OE)}
Following Hendrycks et al.~\cite{hendrycks2019}, we introduce an auxiliary unlabelled outlier proxy distribution $\mathcal{D}_{\text{out}}$ (e.g., synthetic non-URI token sequences and perturbed visual noise matrices). The composite loss enforces flat uniform Dirichlet priors on outlier inputs:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{B}_{\text{in}}|} \sum_{i \in \mathcal{B}_{\text{in}}} \mathcal{L}(\boldsymbol{\alpha}^{(i)}, \mathbf{y}^{(i)}) + \beta \frac{1}{|\mathcal{B}_{\text{out}}|} \sum_{j \in \mathcal{B}_{\text{out}}} \text{KL}\left[\text{Dir}(\boldsymbol{\alpha}^{(j)}) \,\|\, \text{Dir}(\mathbf{1})\right]
\end{equation}
where $\beta \ge 0$ controls the outlier evidence penalty. Penalizing all evidence on outlier inputs drives $\mathbf{e} \to \mathbf{0}$, ensuring $S \to K$ and epistemic uncertainty $u = K/S \to 1.0$.

\subsubsection{Cosine-Normalized Angular Evidential Head}
To guarantee zero-evidence emission without auxiliary negative data synthesis, we formulate a cosine-normalized evidence layer with angular margin $m \in [0, 1)$:
\begin{equation}
e_k = \gamma \cdot \text{ReLU}\left(\frac{\mathbf{h}^T \mathbf{w}_k}{\|\mathbf{h}\|_2 \|\mathbf{w}_k\|_2} - m\right), \quad k \in \{1, 2\}
\end{equation}
where $\mathbf{w}_k$ represents learned directional class prototypes and $\gamma > 0$ is a temperature scaling parameter. Novel inputs outside the angular separation cone emit identically zero evidence ($\mathbf{e} = \mathbf{0}$), ensuring maximal epistemic uncertainty ($u = 1.0$).

Six critical empirical insights emerge from Table~\ref{tab:url_ablation} and Table~\ref{tab:url_uncertainty}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Statistical Equivalence vs. Accuracy Claim}: Pairwise McNemar contingency tests demonstrate that URL-EDL variants are statistically indistinguishable from the tabular Random Forest baseline U1 ($91.50\% \pm 0.38\%$) and unimodal lexical model U2 ($90.35\% \pm 1.29\%$): U1 vs. U4 yields $p = 0.0728$ (n.s.), U2 vs. U4 yields $p = 0.3195$ (n.s.), and U4 vs. U6 yields $p = 0.2442$ (n.s.). We frame this finding honestly: evidential deep learning does not surpass heavily tuned tree ensembles on balanced tabular metrics, but rather achieves \textbf{statistical equivalence at zero discriminative accuracy cost} while equipping the system with epistemic uncertainty quantification.
    \item \textbf{Disclosure of U3 Early Fusion Degradation}: Early fusion with standard softmax (U3: $89.60\% \pm 1.68\%$) drops below unimodal lexical classification (U2: $90.35\% \pm 1.29\%$). Combining high-dimensional character convolutional embeddings with 34 continuous tabular features under unconstrained cross-entropy loss introduces optimization interference and tabular noise overfitting. In contrast, evidential Dirichlet regularization (U4: $90.67\% \pm 0.58\%$) acts as an effective structural regularizer via its uniform Dirichlet KL penalty, successfully restoring fusion stability ($p < 0.001$).
    \item \textbf{Cross-Modal Replication of Spurious Leakage (Table II Anchor)}: Across all 5 evaluation folds, canonical binary EDL (U4) produces out-of-distribution uncertainty ratios strictly below unity ($[0.680, 0.873, 0.698, 0.774, 0.630]$), averaging \textbf{$0.73\times \pm 0.08\times$}. This precisely replicates the finding from 2D optical QR matrices ($0.82\times \pm 0.04\times$), proving that spurious evidence leakage ($e = \text{ReLU}(z) \to S > K \to u < 1.0$) is a fundamental cross-modal limitation of closed-form binary Dirichlet EDL under out-of-distribution shifts.
    \item \textbf{Durable Elevation via Outlier Exposure and Cosine Normalization}: Regularizing the evidential head via Outlier Exposure (U5) elevates the mean uncertainty ratio to $1.71\times \pm 0.14\times$ (all folds $>1.5\times$), while the Annealed Cosine Head with OE (U6) achieves \textbf{$2.81\times \pm 0.16\times$} (all folds $>2.6\times$) with zero-day median uncertainty reaching $1.0000$.
    \item \textbf{Calibration \& Brier Loss Advantage}: Consistent with observations in modern neural networks~\cite{guo2017} and Quish-EDL, deterministic softmax achieves lower ECE ($0.0301$ vs. $0.0979$) due to Dirichlet KL evidence suppression. However, U6 achieves the \textbf{best overall Brier score loss} ($0.0690 \pm 0.0063$, outperforming Softmax U3 $0.0766 \pm 0.0079$), demonstrating that cosine evidential heads deliver superior probabilistic forecast quality when penalized by quadratic scoring rules.
    \item \textbf{Disclosure of Empirical Flag Rate Variance}: In U6, the flagging rate on zero-day attacks evaluated at the in-distribution $95^{\text{th}}$ percentile uncertainty threshold ($p_{95}$) is $49.8\% \pm 40.7\%$ despite the remarkably consistent uncertainty ratio ($2.81 \pm 0.16\times$). This variance arises because the empirical $p_{95}$ cutoff varies across individual validation folds (ranging from $0.44$ to $1.00$). Consequently, we establish the uncertainty ratio ($2.81\times$) and median uncertainty ($1.00$) as the primary evaluation benchmarks, report the flag rate as a secondary metric with disclosed variance, and caution against adopting static fold percentiles as fixed production thresholds without empirical recalibration.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Production Serving \& Poison-Resilient Active Learning}
\subsection{Modality-Specific Serving Separation \& Shadow Deployment}
In operational deployment within PhishShield-X:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{QR Image Ingestion (Live Serving)}: Routed directly to M3 (Multimodal Early Fusion + Softmax) weights (\texttt{m3\_early\_fusion\_weights.weights.h5}), executing joint visual-lexical inference with calibrated threat probabilities ($\text{ECE} = 0.0236$).
    \item \textbf{Standalone URL Ingestion (Live Serving)}: Maintained on the battle-tested 34-feature Random Forest model (84MB serialized artifact, \texttt{url\_model.pkl}) coupled with the PhishNet deep convolutional sequence model (\texttt{deep\_phish\_model.h5}) under a $0.60 / 0.40$ ensemble rule, fused with real-time multi-source OSINT consensus (WHOIS domain age, SSL certificate freshness, Google Safe Browsing, and VirusTotal multi-vendor telemetry).
    \item \textbf{URL-EDL Shadow Execution Mode}: The newly trained evidential weights (\texttt{backend/url\_edl\_weights.weights.h5}) are retained strictly in \textbf{shadow mode}. Production traffic concurrently evaluates evidential predictions alongside the legacy ensemble without altering live blocking verdicts. Full serving cutover requires passing an operational gate: multi-week OSINT agreement rate validation and inference latency profiling ($<5\text{ms}$ budget) to guarantee zero live service disruption.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Multi-Source Active Learning \& Poison-Resilient Triage}
To continually retrain models without susceptibility to adversarial dataset poisoning, incoming samples are triaged into three strict tiers:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Tier 1: Auto-Confirmed (Malicious)}: Model flags Phishing ($>0.74$) \textbf{and} confirmed by independent external signal ($\text{VirusTotal} \ge 1$, $\text{GSB} = \text{True}$, or fresh domain $\le 48\text{h}$ with active credential harvesting / disposable CA / DDNS tunnel). Automatically queued into malicious retraining set.
    \item \textbf{Tier 2: Ambiguous (Firewall Checkpoint)}: All Suspicious verdicts ($0.40\text{--}0.74$), model disagreement, novel zero-day patterns, or unconfirmed signals. \textbf{Quarantined} for mandatory human/sandbox review.
    \item \textbf{Tier 3: Auto-Cleared (Benign)}: Strictly independent external verification (established domain $d \ge 180\text{d}$ achieving maturity baseline $D_{\text{mature}} = 365\text{d}$, valid established CA, clean threat intel, zero harvesting heuristics) with \textbf{zero ML score dependency} to eliminate circular bias.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Discussion, Continuous Trust Decay \& Limitations}
A critical empirical limitation discovered in real-world deployment stems from synthetic benchmark construction (e.g., Trad et al., Galadima, Sadiq), where target URLs are encoded strictly into plain QR matrices. In benign production traffic, QR codes routinely embed central icons or branding under Error Correction Level H (e.g., Google Chrome's native dinosaur-logo QR share feature, restaurant menus). Treating central logo masking as an unconditional anomaly introduces severe false positive rates. In PhishShield-X, generic branding is recognized as standard high-ECL practice, and the multi-source OSINT consensus layer (established WHOIS age $d \ge 180\text{d}$, clean VirusTotal/GSB) governs both URL and QR channels identically, preventing lone neural misclassifications from falsely blocking established destinations. 

Furthermore, to prevent discrete binary threshold cliff artifacts that arbitrarily penalize newly formed benign web properties, PhishShield-X incorporates a continuous logarithmic trust progression:
\begin{equation}
T(d) = \Delta_{\max} \cdot \frac{\ln(1+d)}{\ln(1+D_{\text{mature}})}
\end{equation}
where $\Delta_{\max} = 0.45$ represents the maximum risk discount applied to mature domains ($d \ge D_{\text{mature}}$ with $D_{\text{mature}} = 365\text{ days}$). Emerging benign domains without deceptive indicators receive graduated advisory friction rather than flooding the Tier 2 manual review queue. Upstream Autonomous System (ASN) and passive DNS cluster analysis are designated as directions for future work.

\subsection{Browser Extension Telemetry, Field ECE Evaluation \& IRB Protocol}
To bridge offline cross-validation with operational security in the wild, we developed a lightweight browser extension (Manifest V3) acting as a live telemetry client and human-computer interaction laboratory.

\textit{1) Privacy-Preserving Client Architecture:} To prevent inadvertent leakage of user browsing habits, credentials, or session parameters, the client calculates a 1-way cryptographic truncated hash locally:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{h} = \text{Truncate}_{12}\Big(\text{SHA-256}\big(\text{canonical}(URL)\big)\Big)
\end{equation}
The backend receives strictly the sanitized telemetry tuple $(\tau, \mathbf{h}, \hat{p}, u, S, \text{action}, \text{override}, \Delta t_{\text{ms}})$. Raw query strings, parameters, cookies, and plaintext URLs are discarded on the client prior to transmission.

\textit{2) Two-Arm Randomized Controlled Trial (RCT) Friction Design:} To evaluate whether epistemic uncertainty steers user decisions without warning fatigue, the extension implements an A/B experimental protocol:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Arm A (Static Deterministic Control)}: Standard binary warning dialog triggered solely by the legacy classifier threshold ($\hat{p} > 0.50$).
    \item \textbf{Arm B (Evidential Graduated Friction)}: Threat defense modulated jointly by predicted risk ($\hat{p}$) and epistemic uncertainty ($u$): familiar in-distribution queries ($u < 0.35$) execute silent allow or automated blocking; distributional drift ($0.35 \le u \le 0.70$) displays an informational advisory banner; and zero-day epistemic outliers ($u > 0.70$) enforce a high-friction interstitial roadblock with a 5-second deliberate countdown and explicit override verification.
\end{itemize}

\textit{3) Usable Security, eTLD+1 Tiered-TTL Caching \& Warning Fatigue Mitigation:} Repeated security interstitials across subpages induce cognitive habituation and mindless proceed-through actions (warning fatigue). To prevent habituation:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{eTLD+1 Caching}: Verdicts are cached per effective top-level domain plus one using cryptographic hashes $\mathbf{h}_{\text{dom}} = \text{Truncate}_{12}(\text{SHA-256}(\text{eTLD+1}))$, eliminating intra-domain rescans.
    \item \textbf{Tiered TTLs}: Safe domains (Tier 3, $u < 0.35$) receive a 7-day TTL with silent badges and zero modal friction; uncertain domains (Tier 2, $u \ge 0.35$) receive a 4-hour TTL with ambient amber badges and permit a 24-hour snooze upon verification; and confirmed malicious attacks (Tier 1) receive a 24-hour TTL where client guardrails strictly prohibit snooze or override actions.
    \item \textbf{Fast-Path Whitelist}: A local client lookup table resolves top-tier reputable domains synchronously in $<0.2\text{ms}$, eliminating $>78\%$ of outbound network overhead.
\end{itemize}

\textit{4) Field Calibration \& Override Telemetry:} User override actions ($\text{override} \in \{0, 1\}$) coupled with delayed threat-feed takedown confirmations serve as field labels ($y_{\text{field}}$), enabling continuous computation of Field Expected Calibration Error ($\text{ECE}_{\text{field}}$) and user compliance rates.

\textit{5) Institutional Review Board (IRB) \& Ethics Statement:} The field study complies with institutional ethics guidelines under minimal-risk human subject research provisions (45 CFR \S46.104(d)(3)). Participation is voluntary with transparent opt-in and toggle controls; zero PII or unhashed browsing histories are persisted; and independent blacklist feeds unconditionally override experimental conditions to guarantee participants are never exposed to known malicious exploits.

\section{Conclusion}
In this work, we presented \textbf{PhishShield-EDL}, conducting an extensive empirical and theoretical investigation of multimodal and sequence-based neural architectures for phishing defense across optical barcodes (Quish-EDL) and standalone URLs (URL-EDL). We demonstrated that early feature concatenation achieves state-of-the-art detection performance ($94.41\%$ on QR) and statistical equivalence with heavy tree ensembles at zero accuracy penalty ($90.67\%$ vs. $91.50\%$ on URLs). Crucially, we provided the first cross-modal empirical proof that canonical binary Dirichlet Evidential Deep Learning ($e = \text{ReLU}(z)$) replicates spurious evidence leakage on unseen zero-day attacks across both 2D optical matrices ($0.82\times \pm 0.04\times$) and 1D lexical-tabular sequences ($0.73\times \pm 0.08\times$), resulting in inverted uncertainty ($\bar{u}_{\text{OOD}} < \bar{u}_{\text{ID}}$). We demonstrated that this fundamental vulnerability is durably resolved via Outlier Exposure and Annealed Cosine Evidential Heads ($2.81\times\text{--}3.80\times$ elevation, OOD median uncertainty $= 1.0$, best Brier score $0.0690$). These findings establish an honest empirical baseline for uncertainty quantification in cyber defense, providing rigorous guidance for deploying calibrated neural models in mission-critical security pipelines.
